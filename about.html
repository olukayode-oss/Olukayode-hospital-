Our Story

Olukayode Hospital is a convalescence home designed to provide a comfortable environment for people during a period of recovery.

Recovery is not only about treatment. The environment around a person can also play an important role in their comfort and overall experience. Our website provides a place where visitors can learn more about the hospital and the services it provides.

As this website continues to develop, this section can be expanded with the hospital's real history, founding story, milestones and information about the people behind the organisation.

Our Purpose

The purpose of this page is to give visitors a clearer understanding of Olukayode Hospital beyond a simple contact page.
