Who We Are

Olukayode Hospital is a convalescence home located in Lagos, Nigeria. Our goal is to provide a peaceful, comfortable and supportive environment for people during their recovery.


We understand that recovery is more than simply having a place to stay. Comfort, cleanliness, communication and a supportive environment can all contribute to a better recovery experience.


Our approach is centred around respect, dignity and attention to individual needs. We aim to make visitors and families feel welcome while providing an environment suitable for rest and recovery.


This website provides information about our convalescence home, our services and ways to contact us. For urgent medical emergencies, always contact an appropriate emergency medical service or qualified healthcare professional.
